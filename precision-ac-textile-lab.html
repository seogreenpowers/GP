Why It Matters

The Foundation of Reliable Textile Testing

These precision air conditioning units are able to assure a daily constant climatic control guaranteeing perfect conditions of uniformity at every point of the conditioned testing areas foreseen for the preparation of samples and in order to improve test repeatability and reproducibility.

The most accurate quality control on physical and mechanical properties for Textile products has to be considered the Keyword of the Modern Textile Industry that has to reach the highest scopes of Quality for their products in a competitive market. The tests and analyses on textiles must be performed in environmentally controlled rooms, thus obtaining results more precise, influential and evaluable.

System Capabilities

Precision Control at Every Level

Every parameter engineered to meet the strict requirements of international textile testing standards.

Precise Temperature Control

Maintains 20°C ± 2°C continuously, with rapid recovery after door openings, ensuring conditioning integrity during sample handling.

Humidity Uniformity

Ultrasonic humidification maintains 65% ± 4% RH uniformly throughout the lab, preventing moisture gradients that compromise test results.

Energy Efficiency

EC fan technology and intelligent controls minimize energy consumption while maintaining the strictest environmental parameters.

Intelligent Controls

Vertiv ICOM control system with SNMP/web monitoring, alarm management and remote access for continuous laboratory oversight.

N+1 Redundancy

Redundant unit configurations guarantee continuous conditioning even during maintenance or equipment fault, preventing costly test interruptions.

Standards Compliance

Systems configured to meet ISO 139, ASTM D1776, BS EN ISO 139 and other international textile conditioning standards required for accreditation.
