Online UPS Systems — Common Questions

What UPS capacity do I need for a small office?

Most small offices with a handful of computers and network equipment are well served by a 1–10 kVA single-phase UPS. Green Powers assesses your connected load before recommending an exact size.

Can Green Powers supply UPS systems above 100 kVA?

Yes. We supply and install three-phase Vertiv UPS systems up to 1000 kVA for data centers, factories and large commercial buildings across Tamil Nadu.

Do you provide installation and AMC along with the UPS?

Yes. Every UPS we supply includes professional installation, commissioning and an optional Annual Maintenance Contract with certified Vertiv-trained engineers.
