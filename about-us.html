Our Story

Powering Tamil Nadu's Most Critical Infrastructure

In today's rapidly evolving digital landscape, businesses need more than just technology to stay competitive — they need future-ready infrastructure. At Green Powers, we are deeply passionate about providing innovative infrastructure solutions that keep businesses operational, protected, and ready for growth.

Our team of experts continually researches and integrates the latest trends and technologies, ensuring that every client receives a solution that is not only fit for today but built to scale for tomorrow. From compact office UPS systems to large-scale data center deployments, we handle every project with the same commitment to excellence.

As an authorized dealer for Vertiv, Liebert, and Amararaja Quanta, we bring world-class products backed by local expertise and a service network spanning Chennai, Coimbatore, Madurai, Sivakasi, and Hosur.

Guruprasath M, Managing Director, leads the Green Powers team from our Madurai headquarters, overseeing every installation and AMC engagement to the same standard of reliability our clients have relied on for 15+ years.
