FAQs

Batteries — Common Questions

What is the difference between SMF and VRLA batteries?

Quanta SMF batteries are 12V sealed maintenance-free units (7Ah–200Ah) suited to small and medium UPS systems, while Quanta VRLA 2V cells (100Ah–3000Ah) are built for large UPS and telecom installations needing higher standby capacity.

Do Green Powers batteries come with a warranty?

Yes. All Amararaja Quanta batteries supplied by Green Powers carry the manufacturer's standard warranty, and we handle warranty claims directly on your behalf.

Can you monitor battery health remotely?

Yes. Our Battery Health Monitoring System (BHMS) tracks battery condition in real time so failing cells are replaced proactively, before they cause downtime.
